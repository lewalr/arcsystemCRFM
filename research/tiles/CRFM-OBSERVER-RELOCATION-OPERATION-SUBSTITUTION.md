# CRFM — Observer Relocation and Operation–Representation Substitution

**Recorded:** 2026-10-12  
**Status:** Observed output discrepancy; proposed validation, not verified geometry.

## Prior discoveries
CRFM distinguishes persistent world geometry, observer/reference-frame changes, projected appearances and their validation. Earlier sketch/substrate experiments distinguished applying construction rules from illustrating those rules.

## New discovery
In the black-cube holographic sculpture experiment, a request for an observer to walk around the sculpture generated a large frontal scene plus three arrow-linked smaller viewpoints. It illustrated movement rather than yielding one relocated camera view. The black cube, fingertip-water contact and ripples remained recognizable, while a single observer trajectory and fixed geometry were not established.

## Further insight
**Operation–representation substitution** names the observable failure mode in which a generated image depicts a requested operation instead of delivering its resulting state. Semantic continuity of scene elements is not proof of geometric continuity or an internal persistent 3D model.

## Proposed outcome
Freeze the source scene. Request one view from the rear-right, approximately 120° around the same stationary cube, with no arrows, panels or collages. Compare against a narrated-walk control. Score one-frame compliance, camera displacement, landmark correspondences, occlusion/parallax, lighting, and observer identity separately. Preserve prompt, source, output, failure cases and uncertainty.
